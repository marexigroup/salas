The Core Idea
The original coliving.

The Camino Primitivo has been moving strangers through Salas since Alfonso II walked it in 814 AD. What we now call "coliving" — shared space, shared meals, temporary community with people you would never otherwise meet — the Camino invented it. El Primitivo does not reference that history. It continues it. No other coliving in the world can say that.

Positioning Statement

The one paragraph
For remote workers in their second chapter of nomadism

El Primitivo is Europe's only coliving space on the original Camino de Santiago — eight private houses in a medieval Asturian village, where the 9th-century pilgrimage route runs through the plaza and the castle is the view from the window. For experienced nomads who are done optimising their setup and want somewhere that feels earned: a month of deep work, genuine quiet, and a small community that actually knows each other's names.

Original

1,200 years of welcoming wanderers. The Camino Primitivo, the castle, the village — none of this was built for nomads. It was here already. That authenticity cannot be manufactured and is the core of everything we say and show.

Eight rooms. Everyone knows everyone within 48 hours. No lobby, no reception desk, no stranger you pass in a corridor. Small is not a constraint — it is the product. Never expand beyond what can be personally quality-controlled.

Voice & Tone

How we write — two examples
Write like this

"The Camino Primitivo passes through the plaza. The castle is two minutes' walk. The mountains begin at the edge of town. You have fast internet and a desk."

Declarative. No adjectives that don't earn their place. No "vibrant community" or "inspiring surroundings." Let the facts do the work. Trust the reader to understand what it means without being told how to feel about it.

Not like this

"Discover an amazing coliving experience in a stunning medieval village surrounded by breathtaking Asturian nature, where you can connect with like-minded digital nomads!"

Every word here is generic and could describe anywhere. "Amazing," "stunning," "breathtaking," "like-minded" — these are filler. Exclamation marks signal insecurity. If a sentence could appear in a Selina description, delete it.
